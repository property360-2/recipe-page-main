Simple Omelette Recipe

An easy and quick dish, perfect for any meal. This classic omelette combines beaten eggs cooked to perfection, optionally filled with your choice of cheese, vegetables, or meats.

Preparation Time

  • Total: Approximately 10 minutes
  • Preparation: 5 minutes
  • Cooking: 5 minutes

Ingredients

  • 2-3 large eggs
  • Salt, to taste
  • Pepper, to taste
  • 1 tablespoon of butter or oil
  • Optional fillings: cheese, diced vegetables, cooked meats, herbs

Instructions

  1. Beat the eggs: In a bowl, beat the eggs with a pinch of salt and pepper until well mixed. Optional: add 1 tbsp of water or milk for fluffiness.
  2. Heat the pan: Place a non-stick frying pan over medium heat and add butter or oil.
  3. Cook the omelette: When the butter is bubbling, pour in the eggs. Tilt the pan to evenly coat the surface.
  4. Add fillings: Once the eggs start setting but are still a little runny, sprinkle your fillings over one side.
  5. Fold and serve: Fold the omelette in half and let cook for one more minute. Then slide it onto a plate.
  6. Enjoy: Serve hot, with extra salt and pepper if desired.
